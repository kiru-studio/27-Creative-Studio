# 27 Creative Studio - Cinema-Grade UI/UX Design System

This skill codifies the design language, components, visual hierarchy, and interaction patterns for **27 Creative Studio**'s production operating system and client CRM.

## 1. Aesthetic DNA: Cinema HUD & Operating System
Inspired by **Frame.io v4**, **DaVinci Resolve Studio**, **RED/Sony CineAlta Viewfinders**, and **Linear**:
- **Backgrounds**: Deep Obsidian `#07070A`, Viewfinder Dark `#0C0C12`, Surface Panel `#12121A`, Elevated Slate `#181822`.
- **Borders**: Hairline Cinema Graphite `#22222E`, High-light `#2E2E3E`.
- **Accents**:
  - `Director Neon Lime` (`#C6FF3D`): Active selections, primary actions, positive conversion.
  - `Tally Red` (`#EF4444`): Live filming, recording status, critical call sheet alert.
  - `Amber / Golden Hour` (`#F59E0B`): Call times, scheduled shoots, lighting conditions, deals.
  - `Anamorphic Cyan` (`#38BDF8`): Live social analytics, follower growth, client deliverables.
  - `Grade Magenta` (`#E879F9`): In-edit post-production, DaVinci color grading, audio sound design.
  - `Master Green` (`#22C55E`): Delivered projects, retained clients, verified exports.

## 2. Niche Visual Markers
1. **Viewfinder Framing Brackets**: Subtle corner brackets (`⌜ ⌝ ⌞ ⌟`) on key telemetry cards and preview viewfinders.
2. **Camera Sensor & Rec HUD**:
   - `REC ●` blinking tally indicator with dynamic SMPTE timecode (`00:14:22:18`).
   - Technical badges: `4K DCI 24P`, `S-Cinetone / S-Log3`, `XAVC S-I 10-Bit 4:2:2`, `Shutter 180°`.
3. **Aspect Ratio Indicators**:
   - `9:16 VERTICAL` for Instagram Reels & TikTok viral shorts.
   - `16:9 CINEMA` for Brand Films & YouTube 4K master features.
4. **Post-Production Milestones**:
   - `HOOK [0-3s]`: First 3-second retention hook editing.
   - `A-ROLL`: Primary dialogue/interview cut.
   - `B-ROLL`: High-speed product / food / venue cinematography.
   - `SOUND`: Sound design, Foley, trending commercial music sync.
   - `GRADE`: Sony S-Log3 to Rec.709 cinema grade LUT.
   - `MASTER`: Client ready 4K 60Mbps export.
5. **Call Sheet & Location Logistics**:
   - Coastal Tamil Nadu Golden Hour markers (Sunrise / Sunset times in Thoothukudi & Tirunelveli).
   - Drone flight wind warnings & weather conditions.
   - Flight case gear checklist with verification badges (Sony FX3, Sigma 24-70mm f/2.8, DJI RS3 Pro, Godox SL200, DJI Mic 2).

## 3. Strict UI/UX Rules
- **No Awkward Wrapping**: All button labels and badge chips must use `whitespace-nowrap shrink-0`.
- **Spacious Layouts**: Kanban columns must have fixed adequate width (`min-w-[320px]`) inside a clean horizontal scroll track with zero clipping.
- **Role-Clearance Integrity**: Financial metrics masked for crew/editors; call sheets and flight cases emphasized for crew; post queue emphasized for editors; brand deliverables emphasized for clients.
- **Direct Real-World Actions**: One-click WhatsApp Director link with prefilled project messages, one-click Google Maps pin launch, one-click Call buttons.
