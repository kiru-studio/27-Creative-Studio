========================================================================
05_CASE_STUDIES — RESOLUTION & CONTENT GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE THIS IS USED ON THE SITE:
- The "Proof in the cut / Real Growth. Real Results." case study accordion section.
- Expands to show video proof and result screenshots for client campaigns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CASE STUDY VIDEOS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       16:9 (Landscape) or 9:16 (Vertical)
- Resolution:         1920 x 1080 px (or 1080 x 1920 px)
- Format:             .MP4 (H.264)
- Target File Size:   Under 25 MB
- Target Duration:    15s to 45s showing before-and-after proof, customer reactions, or stats.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CASE STUDY POSTERS / SCREENSHOTS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       16:9 (Landscape)
- Resolution:         1920 x 1080 px
- Format:             .WebP or .JPG
- Target File Size:   Under 200 KB
- Content:            Client showroom photo, dining room crowd, or Instagram growth graphs.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN FIELD:
Go to Admin -> "Case Studies" -> Click Client -> "Case Video URL" & "Case Poster Image".
