02_REELS_SHORTFORM_9x16 / VIDEOS — RESOLUTION & ENCODING GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE THIS IS USED ON THE SITE:
- The interactive "Selected Work / Reels" vertical gallery.
- Opens in the full-screen cinematic mobile reel viewer.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
VERTICAL REEL VIDEO SPECIFICATIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       9:16 (Vertical Portrait)
- Resolution:         1080 x 1920 px (Standard Vertical Full HD)
- Format:             .MP4
- Video Codec:        H.264 (AVC)
- Audio Codec:        AAC Stereo (48kHz, 192 kbps)
- Frame Rate:         24 FPS or 30 FPS (or 60 FPS for ultra-smooth slomo)
- Target Bitrate:     4,000 to 6,000 kbps
- Target File Size:   Under 25 MB per reel for instant smooth playback
- Target Duration:    15 seconds to 45 seconds

PRO-TIP FOR GOOGLE DRIVE:
Google Drive plays videos best when exported with "Fast Start" (MOOV atom at the
beginning of the MP4 file). In Premiere/DaVinci, enable "Web Optimized" or
"Fast Start" in your MP4 export settings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN FIELD:
Go to Admin -> "Reels & Shortform" -> Click Reel -> "Video URL".
