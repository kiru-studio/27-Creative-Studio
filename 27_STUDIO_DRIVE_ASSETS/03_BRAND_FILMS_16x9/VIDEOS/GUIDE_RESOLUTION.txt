03_BRAND_FILMS_16x9 / VIDEOS — RESOLUTION & ENCODING GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE THIS IS USED ON THE SITE:
- The "Films with room to breathe" cinema player section.
- Plays in the 16:9 widescreen cinema showcase with timecode and audio scrub controls.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
BRAND FILM VIDEO SPECIFICATIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       16:9 (Cinematic Widescreen)
- Resolution:         1920 x 1080 px (1080p) or 2560 x 1440 px (2K)
- Format:             .MP4
- Video Codec:        H.264 (AVC)
- Audio Codec:        AAC Stereo (48kHz, 256 kbps)
- Frame Rate:         24 FPS (Cinematic standard)
- Target Bitrate:     5,000 to 8,000 kbps
- Target File Size:   Under 45 MB
- Target Duration:    30 seconds to 90 seconds (00:45 typical)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN FIELD:
Go to Admin -> "Brand Films" -> Click Film -> "16:9 Video URL".
