========================================================================
01_HERO_MEDIA — RESOLUTION & ENCODING GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE THIS IS USED ON THE SITE:
- The skewed parallelogram video window right above the fold on the homepage.
- The "Watch client reels" modal player.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. HERO BACKGROUND LOOP VIDEO:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       16:9 (Landscape)
- Resolution:         1920 x 1080 px (Full HD)  [or 1280 x 720 px for fast load]
- Format:             .MP4
- Video Codec:        H.264 (AVC) - High Profile (Maximum compatibility)
- Audio:              Muted / No audio required (website autoplays muted)
- Frame Rate:         24 FPS (Cinematic standard)
- Bitrate:            3,000 to 5,000 kbps (keep file size under 15 MB)
- Duration:           5 to 15 seconds seamless loop

PRO-TIP: Export a short, high-energy teaser loop showing fast cuts of
food sizzle, car delivery, and camera gimbal movements.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. HERO POSTER FALLBACK IMAGE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       16:9 (Landscape)
- Resolution:         1920 x 1080 px
- Format:             .WebP or .JPG (optimized)
- Target File Size:   Under 250 KB
- Purpose:            Instantly displays while the video loop buffers.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN FIELD:
Go to Admin -> "Studio & General" -> "Hero Video Loop" & "Hero Video Poster".
