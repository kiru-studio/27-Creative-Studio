04_CREW_PORTRAITS_3x4 — RESOLUTION & COMPOSITION GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE THIS IS USED ON THE SITE:
- The "Behind the Camera / Small crew. Big attention." interactive section.
- Displayed directly inside the physical hanging lanyard badges on the rail.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
CREW PORTRAIT SPECIFICATIONS:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       3:4 (Vertical Badge Ratio)
- Resolution:         900 x 1200 px (or 1200 x 1600 px high-res)
- Format:             .WebP (Recommended for sharpness & speed) or .JPG
- Target File Size:   Under 150 KB
- Composition:        Waist-up or chest-up portrait.
                      Subject holding cinema gear (e.g. Sony FX3, DJI gimbal, headphones).
                      Dark moody studio lighting or cinematic production environment.
                      Center framing so head and shoulders align inside the badge window.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
ADMIN FIELD:
Go to Admin -> "Crew Passes" -> Click Member -> "Portrait Photo".
(Adding a member here automatically adds a physical hanging lanyard badge on the homepage!)
