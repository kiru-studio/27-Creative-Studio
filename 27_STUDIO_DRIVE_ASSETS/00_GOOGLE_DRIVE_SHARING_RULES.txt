HOW TO SHARE FILES SO THEY PLAY ON YOUR WEBSITE:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Whenever you upload a video or image to Google Drive and want to use it
in your Admin panel (https://27-creative-studio.vercel.app/admin):

1. Right-click the file in Google Drive.
2. Click "Share" -> "Share".
3. Under "General access", change from "Restricted" to:
   --> "Anyone with the link" <-- (Role: Viewer)
4. Click "Copy link".
5. Paste that link directly into the Admin CMS field!
   (The Admin automatically converts your link into a direct stream URL).
