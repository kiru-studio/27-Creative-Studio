06_BRAND_LOGOS_ICONS — RESOLUTION & TRANSPARENCY GUIDE
========================================================================

WHERE THIS IS USED ON THE SITE:
- Top navigation bar lockup
- Footer brand credits
- Testimonial filmstrip frames
- Clapperboard branding

------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. STUDIO SYMBOL / LOGOMARK:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       1:1 (Square)
- Resolution:         512 x 512 px
- Format:             Transparent .PNG or .WebP (or .SVG)
- Content:            The bold "27" numeral lockup symbol.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. FULL BRAND LOGO:
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Aspect Ratio:       1:1 or 16:9
- Resolution:         1080 x 1080 px or 1920 x 1080 px
- Format:             .PNG, .JPG, or .WebP
- Content:            Full typography: "27 CREATIVE STUDIO".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. CLIENT LOGOS (Optional):
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Resolution:         400 x 200 px (Horizontal) or 300 x 300 px
- Format:             Transparent .PNG (Monochrome or clean contrast on dark background).
